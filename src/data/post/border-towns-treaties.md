The Free Pax States aren't a country. They're a promise between towns.

After the Pax collapse, the border towns had two choices: get absorbed by a neighbor empire, or promise each other mutual defense and free passage. They chose the promise. No standing army. A council of envoys. A shared archive of every treaty, toll, and trade route.

Right now I'm mapping:

- Which towns signed first, and which are still holding out
- The trade corridors that actually matter (salt, paper, spare parts)
- Where the treaties are thinnest — and what happens when one breaks

This is FILE 03 in the field notes. Maps and town entries coming as I finish them.
