Kilumi is the trade tongue of the southern archipelagos. Agglutinative, vowel-harmony, SOV — built for clarity over poetry, though it has plenty of both.

One of my favorite parts so far: evidentiality is mandatory. You can't just say "the shipment arrived." You have to say *how you know*:

- `kilu-mi-na` — I saw it myself (`-na` = seen)
- `kilu-mi-ti` — I heard it from someone (`-ti` = heard)
- `pax-suri-na` — the breach, witnessed. `pax-suri-ti` — the breach, reported.

Traders don't argue about facts. The grammar won't let them.
